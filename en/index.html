A firm rooted in the Cameroonian field

Kaizen Expertise is the trading name of Kaizen Consulting and Services SARL (KCS SARL), founded in January 2017 in Douala. The firm supports companies of all sizes in adopting Kaizen, the Japanese approach to continuous improvement, adapted to the realities of African business.

Our starting point is always the Gemba. Before proposing anything, we observe how work is really done, we listen to the teams, and we build simple, measurable and lasting improvements with them.

The firm has worked in Cameroon, the Central African Republic, Senegal, Mali and Madagascar, alongside institutions such as JICA, UNDP, the ILO and MINPMEESA. It presented the Cameroonian Kaizen experience at the Africa Kaizen Annual Conference (AKAC) in Durban in 2018.

The 5S method: five steps, one after the other

5S is the foundation of every Kaizen project. The name comes from five Japanese words starting with the letter S. You move to the next step only when the previous one is in place.

  1. 1

    Seiri

    Sort

    Separate what is useful from what is not. Anything not needed for daily work leaves the workstation.

  2. 2

    Seiton

    Set in order

    A place for everything and everything in its place, so anything can be found in seconds.

  3. 3

    Seiso

    Shine

    Keep the workstation clean, and use cleaning to spot leaks, wear and anomalies.

  4. 4

    Seiketsu

    Standardise

    Write the rules down and make them visible, so everyone works the same way.

  5. 5

    Shitsuke

    Sustain

    Build discipline: regular checks, clear roles and a culture of continuous improvement.

Expected result: less waste, less time lost searching, more safety, and teams who take ownership of their workspace.
